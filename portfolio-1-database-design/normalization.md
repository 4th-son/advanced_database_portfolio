The MarketLink relational database was designed using the principles of database normalisation to reduce data redundancy and improve data integrity. First Normal Form was achieved by ensuring that attributes contain atomic values. Second Normal Form was addressed by separating information that depends on only part of a composite key into appropriate entities. Third Normal Form was achieved by separating independent entities, such as customers, vendors and product categories, into individual tables. The resulting design improves maintainability and reduces unnecessary duplication while preserving relationships through primary and foreign keys.
